# JetSetGo - Booking, Fare and Travel Policy

Version 4.2 - Effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Scope and Definitions

1.1 This policy applies to all tickets issued by JetSetGo ("JetSetGo", "we", "us") on flights operated by JetSetGo, whether booked on jetsetgo.com, through the JetSetGo mobile app, through our call centre, or through a travel agent.

1.2 "Departure" means the scheduled departure time of the first flight on the ticket. "Travel" means any flight on the ticket. A ticket is "partially used" once the first flight has been flown.

1.3 "Travel credit" is a non-cash credit, held in the passenger's name, that can be used towards a future JetSetGo booking. Travel credit is valid for 12 months from the date it is issued and cannot be transferred to another person or exchanged for cash.

1.4 If information given through any JetSetGo channel (website, app, call centre, airport staff or the digital assistant) appears to conflict with this policy, please contact Customer Care before you travel or make a purchase so we can confirm the correct rule. JetSetGo is responsible for the information provided through all of its channels, including its digital assistant.

## 2. Fare Types

2.1 JetSetGo sells four fare types: Basic, Standard, Flex and Business.

2.2 Basic fares include one personal item only. Seat selection, checked baggage and changes are not included. Basic fares are non-refundable and cannot be changed after the 24-hour risk-free period described in section 4.

2.3 Standard fares include one carry-on bag, one personal item and standard seat selection. Changes are permitted for a change fee of USD 75 per passenger per direction plus any fare difference. Standard fares are non-refundable, but if you cancel before departure the unused value, minus a USD 75 cancellation fee, is returned as travel credit.

2.4 Flex fares include one carry-on bag, one personal item, one checked bag up to 23 kg, and seat selection. Changes are free of charge; only any fare difference applies. Flex fares are fully refundable to the original form of payment if cancelled before departure.

2.5 Business fares include two checked bags up to 32 kg each, lounge access where available, and priority boarding. Changes are free of charge; only any fare difference applies. Business fares are fully refundable to the original form of payment if cancelled before departure.

2.6 Taxes and government fees on a cancelled, unused ticket are refunded to the original form of payment for all fare types, including Basic, if the passenger requests it within 12 months of the ticket issue date.

## 3. Booking and Payment

3.1 Bookings can be made up to 330 days before departure.

3.2 We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and JetSetGo travel credit. Cash payments are accepted only at JetSetGo airport ticket counters.

3.3 Fares are only guaranteed once payment has been completed and a ticket number has been issued. A booking that is held without payment is released automatically after 24 hours.

3.4 The name on the booking must match the passenger's passport or government-issued photo ID exactly.

3.5 Name corrections of up to three characters (for example, a spelling mistake) are free of charge. A ticket cannot be transferred to a different person.

3.6 Fares are quoted in the currency of the country of departure. Any currency conversion fees charged by your bank or card issuer are not refunded by JetSetGo.

## 4. 24-Hour Risk-Free Cancellation

4.1 Any ticket, including Basic fares, can be cancelled for a full refund to the original form of payment within 24 hours of purchase, provided the booking was made at least 7 days before departure.

4.2 Bookings made less than 7 days before departure are not eligible for the 24-hour risk-free cancellation.

4.3 Tickets purchased through a travel agent must be cancelled through that agent.

## 5. Changing a Booking

5.1 Changes can be made on jetsetgo.com, in the app or through Customer Care until 2 hours before departure.

5.2 When you change a flight, you pay any applicable change fee (see section 2) plus the difference between the original fare and the new fare. If the new fare is lower, the difference is not refunded, except on Flex and Business fares where it is returned as travel credit.

5.3 Same-day standby is available for Standard, Flex and Business fares for USD 50 per passenger, subject to seat availability. Same-day standby is not available on Basic fares.

5.4 Changes made through our call centre carry an additional service fee of USD 25 per booking. This fee is waived for passengers who require special assistance.

## 6. Cancellations and Refunds

6.1 To cancel a ticket, use Manage Booking on jetsetgo.com or contact Customer Care before departure.

6.2 Refund eligibility depends on the fare type, as set out in section 2. Refunds to the original form of payment are processed within 7 business days for card payments and within 20 business days for other payment methods.

6.3 Partially used tickets: if you cancel the remaining flights on a partially used ticket, only Flex and Business fares are eligible for a refund of the unused portion. For Basic and Standard fares, the unused portion is not refundable, except for taxes and government fees as described in 2.6.

6.4 Refund requests for eligible tickets must be submitted within 12 months of the ticket issue date. Requests received after this period will not be accepted.

6.5 Refunds are always made to the original form of payment, or as travel credit where this policy says so. JetSetGo does not issue refunds in cash or to a different card.

6.6 If JetSetGo cancels your flight, or changes its departure time by more than 3 hours, you may choose either a free rebooking on the next available JetSetGo flight or a full refund to the original form of payment, regardless of fare type.

## 7. Bereavement and Compassionate Fares

7.1 JetSetGo offers reduced bereavement fares for passengers travelling because of the death or imminent death of an immediate family member. Immediate family means a spouse or partner, parent, child, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or legal guardian.

7.2 Bereavement fares must be requested and booked through Customer Care before travel begins. They cannot be booked online.

7.3 Bereavement fares cannot be applied retroactively. We cannot refund the difference between a regular fare and a bereavement fare for travel that has already started or been completed, and we do not accept bereavement fare requests after the ticket has been used.

7.4 At the time of booking, the passenger must provide the name of the deceased or ill family member, their relationship to the passenger, and the name and phone number of the funeral home, hospital or attending physician. JetSetGo may ask for a death certificate or a letter from the physician.

7.5 Bereavement fares are available in Standard and Business cabins only, for travel within 14 days of the booking date. They follow the change rules of a Flex fare but are non-refundable.

7.6 If you have already bought a regular ticket before travel begins, Customer Care can exchange it for a bereavement fare before your first flight. Any fare difference is returned as travel credit.

## 8. Baggage

8.1 Carry-on bag: maximum 7 kg and 55 x 40 x 23 cm. Personal item: maximum 40 x 30 x 15 cm, for example a handbag or small laptop bag. Basic fares include the personal item only; a carry-on bag on a Basic fare costs USD 35 per direction when added online, or USD 60 at the airport.

8.2 Checked baggage allowance depends on the fare type (see section 2). Additional checked bags cost USD 40 for the first extra bag and USD 75 for each further bag, per direction, when purchased online at least 24 hours before departure. At the airport the price is USD 60 and USD 100.

8.3 Overweight bags: bags between 23 kg and 32 kg are charged USD 100 per bag per direction. Bags over 32 kg are not accepted as checked baggage and must be shipped as cargo.

8.4 Sports equipment and musical instruments count towards the checked baggage allowance. Instruments larger than the carry-on size may travel in the cabin only if a seat is purchased for them.

8.5 Delayed or damaged baggage must be reported at the JetSetGo baggage desk at the arrival airport before leaving the baggage hall, or within 7 days of arrival for damage and within 21 days of the bag being returned for delay. Passengers whose checked baggage is delayed may claim reasonable interim expenses, such as toiletries and essential clothing, up to USD 50 per day for a maximum of 5 days, with receipts.

8.6 Lost baggage: a bag is considered lost if it has not been found 21 days after the flight. Claims for lost baggage must be submitted within 30 days of the flight.

## 9. Flight Delays, Cancellations and Compensation

9.1 If your flight is delayed by more than 2 hours, JetSetGo will provide a meal voucher at the airport.

9.2 If your flight is delayed by more than 5 hours, you may choose not to travel and receive a full refund to the original form of payment, regardless of fare type.

9.3 If a delay or cancellation within JetSetGo's control requires an overnight stay, JetSetGo provides hotel accommodation and ground transport between the airport and the hotel.

9.4 For delays within JetSetGo's control that result in arrival at your final destination more than 3 hours late, you may claim compensation of USD 150 for flights under 1,500 km and USD 300 for flights of 1,500 km or more. Compensation claims must be submitted within 12 months of the flight.

9.5 No compensation is payable for delays or cancellations caused by circumstances outside JetSetGo's control, including weather, air traffic control restrictions, security threats, and government orders. Meal, hotel and refund rights in 9.1, 9.2 and 9.3 still apply where stated.

## 10. Overbooking and Denied Boarding

10.1 If a flight is overbooked, JetSetGo will first ask for volunteers to give up their seats in exchange for compensation agreed at the airport.

10.2 Passengers who are denied boarding involuntarily and hold a confirmed ticket, having checked in on time, receive USD 400 if they arrive at their destination 1 to 4 hours later than planned, and USD 800 if they arrive more than 4 hours later, plus a seat on the next available flight.

## 11. Check-in and No-Shows

11.1 Online check-in opens 24 hours and closes 90 minutes before departure. Airport check-in closes 60 minutes before departure for domestic flights and 75 minutes before departure for international flights.

11.2 Boarding gates close 20 minutes before departure.

11.3 If you do not show up for a flight without cancelling or changing it before departure, all remaining flights on the booking are cancelled automatically. No-show Basic and Standard tickets have no remaining value. No-show Flex and Business tickets keep their value as travel credit, minus a USD 100 no-show fee.

## 12. Children and Unaccompanied Minors

12.1 Infants under 2 years may travel on an adult's lap for 10% of the adult fare, plus taxes. One lap infant is allowed per adult.

12.2 Children aged 2 to 11 pay the full adult fare and must have their own seat.

12.3 Children aged 5 to 11 may travel alone using the Unaccompanied Minor service, which costs USD 100 per direction and must be booked through Customer Care at least 48 hours before departure. Children under 5 cannot travel alone. Passengers aged 12 to 17 may use the service as an option.

## 13. Special Assistance

13.1 Wheelchair assistance, assistance for visually or hearing-impaired passengers and other special assistance are provided free of charge. Please request assistance at least 48 hours before departure.

13.2 Mobility aids, including wheelchairs, are carried free of charge and do not count towards the baggage allowance.

13.3 Trained service dogs travel in the cabin free of charge when supporting a passenger with a disability. The required documentation must be submitted at least 48 hours before departure.

## 14. Pets

14.1 Small cats and dogs may travel in the cabin in a soft-sided carrier no larger than 45 x 35 x 20 cm, with a combined weight of pet and carrier of up to 8 kg. The fee is USD 95 per direction.

14.2 Pets in the cabin must be booked through Customer Care. A maximum of two pets are accepted per flight.

14.3 JetSetGo does not carry pets in the cargo hold. Emotional support animals are treated as pets.

## 15. Complaints and Claims

15.1 Complaints can be submitted through the Customer Care form on jetsetgo.com. We respond within 30 days.

15.2 Customer Care can be reached at support@jetsetgo.com or 1800-5555, 24 hours a day.

15.3 Exceptions to this policy can only be granted by a JetSetGo Customer Care supervisor, in writing.
